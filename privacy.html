Privacy

Swag County is a static landing page. We collect anonymous page-view counts and nothing that identifies you.

Effective 2026-10-31

What this site collects

Swag County (the home page at swagcounty.com) is a single static HTML page. There are no forms, no signups and no advertising. The page itself collects nothing. Cloudflare, which sits in front of the site, adds two small scripts, described below.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. For each page view it records the page address, the referring page, your browser, operating system and device type, your country (from your IP address), and how long the page took to load. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and does not fingerprint you or follow you across sites. We see only totals, never individual visitors.

Bot protection

Cloudflare also runs a short check in your browser to tell people from automated traffic. It sets one cookie, cf_clearance, which lasts up to a year and holds only the result of that check. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Server logs

Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, user agent, request path, HTTP status) for debugging and abuse prevention. These logs are retained for a limited period and are not used to build a profile of you or shared with third parties.

Cookies and storage

The only cookie is Cloudflare's cf_clearance bot-check cookie described above. Nothing is written to localStorage or sessionStorage, and no service worker is registered.

Sub-projects

Links from this page may go to interactive sub-projects. Sub-projects that handle user data publish their own privacy policy on their own page. Read the policy for the sub-project you are using — it will be more specific than this one.

Third parties

Apart from the two Cloudflare scripts above, Swag County embeds no third-party scripts, ads or tracking pixels. Cloudflare handles that data under its own privacy policy. The page uses social-preview metadata (Open Graph and Twitter Card) so links shared on other platforms render with an image and title; those platforms fetch the image directly from this site and we receive no information back about who shared it.

Your rights

Apart from short-lived server logs, we hold no personal data about you, and analytics reach us only as totals. There is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. If you are in the EU, UK, or California and want to exercise a data right anyway (right of access, erasure, etc.), email us at the address below and we will confirm in writing that we hold no records about you.

Contact

Privacy questions, data requests, or anything else: nerds@swagcounty.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Swag County handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will move forward. The change history is visible in the public Git history of the repository.
